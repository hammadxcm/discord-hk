# 📋 Discord HK Server Rules

Welcome to our Discord server! To ensure everyone has a positive and enjoyable experience, please follow these rules and guidelines.

## 🛡️ Core Rules

### 1. Respect All Members
- Treat everyone with kindness and respect
- No harassment, bullying, or personal attacks
- Respect different opinions, backgrounds, and cultures
- Use appropriate language and tone

### 2. No Spam or Self-Promotion
- Avoid repetitive messages or flooding channels
- No excessive use of CAPS LOCK or special characters
- Self-promotion is only allowed in designated channels
- No advertisement of other Discord servers without permission

### 3. Keep Content Appropriate
- No NSFW (Not Safe For Work) content anywhere on the server
- No graphic violence, disturbing imagery, or inappropriate content
- Keep discussions family-friendly
- No controversial political or religious debates

### 4. Use Channels Appropriately
- Read channel descriptions and use them for their intended purpose
- Keep conversations relevant to the channel topic
- Use #general for general discussions
- Use #bot-commands for bot interactions

### 5. No Toxicity or Drama
- No toxic behavior, trolling, or intentional disruption
- Don't bring external drama into the server
- Report issues to moderators instead of escalating conflicts
- Keep discussions constructive and positive

## 🚫 Prohibited Content & Behavior

### Strictly Forbidden:
- **Hate Speech**: Any form of discrimination based on race, gender, religion, sexuality, etc.
- **Doxxing**: Sharing personal information of others without consent
- **Threats**: Real or implied threats of violence or harm
- **Illegal Content**: Piracy, illegal downloads, or illegal activities
- **Scams**: Fraudulent schemes or malicious links
- **Alt Accounts**: Using alternate accounts to evade punishments
- **Impersonation**: Pretending to be someone else (staff, celebrities, etc.)
- **Raiding**: Coordinating attacks on other servers or communities

### Content Restrictions:
- No NSFW content (including suggestive avatars/usernames)
- No gore, violence, or disturbing imagery
- No excessive profanity or vulgar language
- No controversial political content
- No religious proselytizing or debates
- No conspiracy theories or misinformation

## 💬 Communication Guidelines

### Voice Channels:
- Use push-to-talk or ensure your mic isn't picking up background noise
- Don't play music unless it's a designated music channel
- Respect others when they're speaking
- Keep conversations appropriate and inclusive

### Text Channels:
- Use proper spelling and grammar when possible
- Don't overuse emojis or reactions
- Spoiler tag content that might spoil movies, games, etc.
- Use threads for extended discussions

### Direct Messages:
- Don't spam members with unsolicited DMs
- Respect if someone doesn't want to be contacted privately
- Report inappropriate DMs to moderators

## 🎮 Gaming & Activities

### Gaming Channels:
- Be respectful in voice chat during games
- No cheating, hacking, or exploiting in games
- Don't be overly competitive or toxic
- Help new players learn instead of criticizing

### Events & Activities:
- Follow event-specific rules posted by organizers
- Be punctual for scheduled events
- Participate in good faith and don't disrupt activities
- Respect event hosts and their decisions

## 🤖 Bot Usage

### General Bot Rules:
- Don't spam bot commands
- Use #bot-commands for testing commands
- Don't try to break or abuse bot features
- Report bot issues to staff

### Economy System:
- No cheating or exploiting the economy system
- Don't create alt accounts for economic advantage
- Trading with real money is prohibited
- Report economic bugs instead of exploiting them

## 👮 Moderation & Enforcement

### Warning System:
1. **First Offense**: Verbal warning
2. **Second Offense**: Official warning + temporary mute
3. **Third Offense**: Temporary ban (1-7 days)
4. **Fourth Offense**: Permanent ban

*Note: Severe violations may result in immediate bans regardless of warning count.*

### Appeal Process:
- Appeals can be submitted through our ticket system
- Provide your Discord ID and reason for appeal
- Appeals are reviewed by senior staff
- Decisions are final after review

### Staff Authority:
- Staff decisions are final
- Don't argue with staff in public channels
- Use tickets or DMs to discuss moderation actions
- Respect all staff members and their roles

## 🎖️ Roles & Permissions

### Earning Roles:
- **Active Member**: Regular participation for 1 week
- **Veteran**: Active member for 1 month
- **Elite**: Exceptional contribution to the community
- **VIP**: Special recognition or Nitro boost

### Role Responsibilities:
- Higher roles come with additional responsibilities
- Help new members and maintain server quality
- Report rule violations to staff
- Set a positive example for others

## 📊 Server Features

### Leveling System:
- Gain XP through active participation
- XP is earned through messages and voice activity
- Don't spam for XP (anti-spam measures in place)
- Level rewards include roles and perks

### Verification:
- New members must verify to access most channels
- Verification helps prevent raids and spam
- Contact staff if you have verification issues

## 🎁 Events & Giveaways

### Participation Rules:
- One entry per person unless specified otherwise
- Must be an active member to participate
- Follow all giveaway requirements
- No begging for giveaways or special treatment

### Event Guidelines:
- Attend events you sign up for
- Be respectful during community events
- Follow event-specific rules
- Thank organizers and participants

## 🔒 Privacy & Safety

### Personal Information:
- Never share personal information publicly
- Be cautious about sharing social media accounts
- Report if someone asks for personal information
- Use privacy settings to protect yourself

### Safety Guidelines:
- Don't meet strangers from Discord in real life
- Be skeptical of suspicious links or downloads
- Report threatening or concerning behavior
- Trust your instincts about uncomfortable situations

## 📱 Platform-Specific Rules

### Stream Channels:
- Only advertise your own content in designated channels
- No NSFW streams or inappropriate content
- Include content warnings for mature games
- Respect copyright and intellectual property

### Social Media:
- Server-related social media should be appropriate
- Don't use server-related accounts for personal drama
- Represent the community positively

## ⚖️ Consequences

### Punishments May Include:
- **Verbal Warning**: For minor infractions
- **Mute**: Temporary restriction from chatting (1 hour - 7 days)
- **Kick**: Removal from server (can rejoin)
- **Temporary Ban**: Cannot rejoin for specified time (1-30 days)
- **Permanent Ban**: Permanent removal from server

### Factors Considered:
- Severity of violation
- Previous infractions
- Intent and context
- Impact on community
- Member's history and contributions
